How the feed works.

Where does the content actually come from?

Every pillar pulls from a real, live source: Sports from ESPN, Anime from AniList, Pop Culture from TMDB/GNews/Giphy, Fashion and Current Events from GNews, and Music from Apple Music and Deezer blended into our own chart. Nothing on the site is written or invented by us — we pull the data live and build the breakdown around it.

How often does it update?

In real time — every page fetches fresh data the moment you load it. There's no publishing schedule or cached snapshot sitting behind it.

Is NewSociety affiliated with ESPN, TMDB, Apple, or any of the sources you pull from?

No. We're an independent aggregator — we credit every source on its detail page (look for the small "Source:" line), and every card links back to the original if you want it straight from them.

Can I save stories to read later?

Yes — tap the ☆ on any card, anywhere on the site, and it's added to your Saved page. That list lives in your browser's local storage, not an account, so it's private to this device and won't follow you to another browser.

How do I search across everything at once?

The search bar in the top nav searches all six pillars at the same time and shows a few results from each. Each pillar's own page also has a deeper, dedicated search if you want more results from just one.

Do you have a mobile app?

Not yet — the site itself is fully responsive, so it works the same way on your phone's browser.

Something looks broken or a story seems off — what do I do?

Since everything's pulled live, an occasional hiccup from an upstream source can happen. Give the page a refresh, and if it's still off, reach out on X.

// newsociety live

Events, venues & getting involved.

What is NewSociety Live?

It's the events and production side of NewSociety — free community nights to ticketed shows, launching in NYC / NJ, plus production services for other people's events. Start at the Live page.

Is the launch party free?

Yes — it's free and RSVP-only. The date and venue haven't been announced yet; RSVP and you'll hear the details by email before anyone else.

Is there an age requirement?

That depends on the venue, so it'll be posted along with the date and location. The RSVP asks for an age range so we can plan around who's coming.

Can I bring people?

Count them in your RSVP — it asks how many people total are coming, including you, so one RSVP covers your group.

What's the VIP option?

We're gauging interest in a VIP option with a reserved area and early access. Nothing's for sale yet — ticking the box on the RSVP just tells us you'd want it.

What happens with my info?

It's used to plan NewSociety Live events and to send you updates about them — no spam, and you'll only hear from us about NewSociety Live.

I run a venue. How do we work together?

Tell us about your space on Host a Night — what kind of room it is, capacity, and which nights you'd like to fill — and we'll reach out to talk dates and terms.

I'm crew, an artist, a brand, or press. Where do I start?

The Get Involved page has one form for all of it — pick what fits and it asks the right questions. Press can also grab assets from the press kit.

Can NewSociety Live produce my event?

Yes — crewing, technical production, and on-site execution for networks, brands, and promoters. Start on Production Services.
