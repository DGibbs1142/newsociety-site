NewSociety

NewSociety is a culture outlet covering sports, anime, pop culture, fashion, music, and the news cycle — built for the feed, not the broadcast booth. Every beat pulls live from the sources that track it and rebuilds the story into its own breakdown, so the coverage moves at the speed the conversation does.

NewSociety Live

NewSociety Live brings the feed into the room — free community nights to ticketed shows, accessible to everyone, launching in NYC / NJ. It also provides crewing, technical production, and on-site execution for networks, brands, and promoters.
